A simple follow-up to the globe and the puzzle map of the world. A two-hemisphere map with the name of each continent supports the lesson and works as a reference card. On the worksheet, the same map has a number on each of the seven continents. Children find each number, say the continent's name, and trace it on handwriting lines with true dotted letters (the dots follow the middle of each stroke, in a print font with a single-story a). The numbers use the Montessori continent colors: North America orange, South America pink, Africa green, Europe red, Asia yellow, Australia brown and Antarctica white. An answer key lets children check their own work.

The map was created with the help of AI tools and checked by hand by a Montessori teacher.
