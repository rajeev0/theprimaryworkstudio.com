Bring penguins to your language shelf with real photographs. Children learn the names of 10 penguins from around the world (emperor, king, Adélie, chinstrap, gentoo, macaroni, African, Magellanic, little blue and Galápagos) and 8 parts of a penguin (head, eye, beak, neck, flipper, belly, feet, tail). Each set has control cards, picture cards and label cards for classic Montessori 3-part card work, plus a control chart. Two half-page booklets: a reading booklet for the shelf and "My Parts of a Penguin Book", where children trace and write each word. A "Penguins of the World" chart shows where each penguin lives.

Real photographs (public domain / CC0). Print font with single-story a and g for beginning readers.
